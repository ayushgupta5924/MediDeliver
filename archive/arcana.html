About

A practice built on presence

I don’t predict your fate—I reflect your reality. Tarot is a tool to slow you down long enough to hear what your intuition already knows.

The Foundation: Deeply trained in the classic Rider-Waite-Smith tradition.

The Style: Blended with modern, intuitive methods.

The Vibe:Direct, compassionate, and grounded in what is actually useful to your life.

Every reading is a collaborative conversation. You bring the question; we find the answers together.

How it works

Simple from start to finish

01

Book your session

Choose a reading type and a time that works for you. You'll receive a confirmation with everything you need to prepare.

02

Bring your question

You don't need a polished question — sometimes an uneasy feeling is enough. We'll find the real question together at the start of the reading.

03

We read together

Sessions are via video call or in person. I'll draw the cards, share what I see, and open the space for your interpretation and reaction.

04

Take it with you

You'll receive a written summary of the cards drawn and key themes within 24 hours, so insights don't fade.
